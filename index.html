Ian Foster

Computer scientist, writer, sailor

Ian Foster is the Arthur Holly Compton Distinguished Service Professor of Computer Science at the University of Chicago and a Senior Scientist and Distinguished Fellow at Argonne National Laboratory, where he directs the Data Science and Learning Division.

He is widely recognized as a founder of grid computing, the distributed computing paradigm that laid the groundwork for today’s cloud infrastructure. For three decades he has built systems that enable scientific collaboration and discovery on a global scale.
